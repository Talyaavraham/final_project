Three trained security detectors against each other (2026-11-26), and what runs by default.

  security.pt            2026-11-10: CCTV guns (Hugging Face) + D-Fire. In the repo.
  security_v3.pt         2026-11-25, Maya: continues security.pt with Dangerous Items (Zenodo) + CCTV-look copies. In the repo.
  security_roboflow.pt   2026-11-26, Talya: the 2026-11-10 recipe (train/colab_train_security.ipynb) + the Roboflow export
                         "CCTV Weapon Detect" v16 AS DOWNLOADED (not train/security/guns_cctv.zip: Drive did not mount and
                         the raw zip was uploaded in cell 4). 25 epochs, yolov8s, imgsz 800. Not in the repo.
                         Its training set holds frames of 16 of the 107 benchmark videos (9 of the 34 with a weapon
                         window), so it cannot be scored on the benchmark. The held-out videos are not in it.

Decision: the default stays  security.pt=fire+smoke, security_v3.pt=gun+knife  (combination A below).
security_roboflow.pt finds far more of the small labelled CCTV pistols, but on the only clean video test (held-out,
14 weapon windows) it cannot be told apart from security_v3.pt, and a detector that saw benchmark videos must not be in
the pipeline the research numbers come from. It can be added by hand:  LIVE_EXTRA / EXTRA_MODELS =
security.pt=fire+smoke,security_v3.pt=gun+knife,security_roboflow.pt=gun  (combination B: most weapon windows found,
most windows returned). Next: the same training on guns_cctv.zip (benchmark videos left out), which can then be measured.
